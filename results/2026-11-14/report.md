## Methodology

- **Benchmarks**: Geekbench 6 single-core, multi-core and Metal scores. One Geekbench version is used throughout.
- **Normalisation**: Each metric is divided by the best value of that metric among the listings ranked under the profile, giving zero to one.
- **Capability score** (zero to 100): the sum of each normalised metric multiplied by its weight.
- **Value score**: capability score divided by price, multiplied by 1,000 (capability per £1,000). This is the ranking key. Ties break on higher capability score, then lower price, then listing id.
- **Chip notation**: "12/16-core" means a 12-core CPU and a 16-core GPU.
- **Model memory headroom**: RAM minus 12 GB. **Usable storage**: SSD minus 150 GB. 12 GB of RAM is reserved for macOS, Docker and Xcode. 150 GB of SSD is reserved for macOS, Xcode, simulators and Docker images.
- **GPU and bandwidth**: 50% normalised Metal score and 50% normalised memory bandwidth.
- **Floors**: at least 16 GB RAM and 512GB SSD. Listings below a floor are excluded.
- **Model classes** (largest model that fits at 4-bit quantisation):
  - 7B: at least 6 GB headroom. About 4.5 GB of weights plus context and runtime.
  - 14B: at least 11 GB headroom. About 9 GB of weights plus context and runtime.
  - 32B: at least 22 GB headroom. About 19 GB of weights plus context and runtime.
  - 70B: at least 45 GB headroom. About 40 GB of weights plus context and runtime.
  - 120B+: at least 75 GB headroom. About 68 GB of weights plus context and runtime.

## Caveats

- Benchmarks measure short synthetic workloads. Sustained performance, thermal limits and real build or inference speed can differ.
- Stock and prices change quickly, especially refurbished stock. Check the source listing before buying.
- Warranty and returns differ between new, Apple refurbished and third-party refurbished machines.
- Desktop prices exclude a display, keyboard and pointing device. Laptop prices include them.
- Colour and display-finish variants of the same configuration are collapsed to the cheapest.
